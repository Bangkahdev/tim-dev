Required production assets to source/create later:

- PBR material sets for porcelain, carpet, wood veneer, glass, aluminum and fabric.
- Office ambience, printer, keyboard, coffee machine, footsteps and meeting-room audio.
- Company logo vector source and room signage artwork.
