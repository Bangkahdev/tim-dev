BANGKAH / HEADQUARTERS
GODOT ENVIRONMENT v0.4

Walk into the company.

Bangkah HQ is designed as a real, walkable workplace. The office is the main interface; Company Command Center and document panels are opened from physical objects inside the building.

Visual direction concept art only. This is not a screenshot captured from the Godot runtime. The included Godot project uses a procedural environment that must be playtested in Godot 4.3+.
Reception / Lobby

Front desk for Mira, visitor seating, glass entrance, feature wall and department wayfinding.

Owner Suite

Executive workstation, monitors, visitor seating and physical incoming Paper tray.

Engineering

Leadership, architecture, frontend, QA, backend and API / infrastructure rooms.

Boardroom

Large meeting table, chairs, wall display and whiteboard.

Archive & Print

Document shelves, reports, printer and archive counter.

Pantry & Lounge

Kitchen run, coffee station, refrigerator, island seating and staff sofas.

Single-floor headquarters, approximately 84 m × 80 m. See docs/ENVIRONMENT-DESIGN.md for space program and interaction points.

Third-person · Default

Over-the-shoulder camera keeps the Owner avatar visible while exploring the office. Mouse wheel adjusts follow distance.

First-person · Toggle V

Camera at Owner eye height, subtle viewmodel sleeves/hands, minimal reticle and interaction prompt. The office fills the screen.

Physical interaction

Look at a nearby target, wait for the contextual prompt, then press E. Panels open only from relevant office objects.

This page is a static design preview. The interactive environment runs from godot/project.godot.
